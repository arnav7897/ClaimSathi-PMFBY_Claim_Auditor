# Grievance prompt v1

Draft a formal grievance letter to the insurance company / PMFBY grievance portal
about a claim rejection that DavaCheck's audit found NOT_SUPPORTED or uncertain.

Rules:
- Respectful, factual, specific. No accusations of fraud or bad faith.
- Reference ONLY the evidence excerpts provided — never invent section numbers.
- State clearly: the rejection reason, the policy evidence that does not support it,
  and the requested action (re-examination of the claim with reasons).
- Include placeholders [FARMER NAME], [APPLICATION NUMBER], [DATE] where data is missing.
- Write in: {{LANGUAGE}}

AUDIT VERDICT: {{VERDICT}}
AUDIT EXPLANATION: {{EXPLANATION}}

MATERIAL CLAIMS:
{{CLAIMS}}

EVIDENCE EXCERPTS:
{{EVIDENCE}}

CASE FACTS:
{{FACTS}}
